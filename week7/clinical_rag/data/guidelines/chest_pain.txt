CHEST PAIN TRIAGE AND MANAGEMENT GUIDELINE
Source: ACC/AHA 2021 Chest Pain Guideline (DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001029)
Last Updated: 2021

=============================================================
SECTION 1: INITIAL TRIAGE — EMERGENCY vs NON-EMERGENCY
=============================================================

Immediate life-threatening causes of chest pain must be ruled out first:
- Acute Myocardial Infarction (STEMI / NSTEMI)
- Aortic Dissection
- Pulmonary Embolism
- Tension Pneumothorax
- Cardiac Tamponade

EMERGENCY CRITERIA — Activate Emergency Response Immediately:
- Chest pain with ST-elevation on ECG → STEMI protocol, target door-to-balloon < 90 minutes
- Chest pain + hypotension (SBP < 90 mmHg)
- Chest pain + severe dyspnea + SpO2 < 90%
- Chest pain + diaphoresis + pallor
- Chest pain + syncope or near-syncope
- Chest pain radiating to jaw, left arm, or back with diaphoresis

HIGH-RISK FEATURES (admit for observation):
- Typical anginal pattern (substernal pressure, exertional, relieved by nitroglycerin)
- Prior history of CAD, MI, or revascularization
- TIMI Risk Score ≥ 3
- HEART Score ≥ 4
- Elevated troponin (any value above 99th percentile URL)
- New or presumed new LBBB on ECG
- Age > 65 with diabetes or hypertension

=============================================================
SECTION 2: DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP
=============================================================

Mandatory Initial Workup:
1. 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of presentation
2. High-sensitivity troponin (hs-cTnI or hs-cTnT) at 0h and 1h (or 0h and 3h)
3. Vital signs: BP (both arms), HR, SpO2, RR, Temperature
4. IV access, continuous cardiac monitoring, supplemental oxygen if SpO2 < 94%
5. Chest X-ray (PA and lateral)
6. Basic metabolic panel, CBC, coagulation studies

Additional workup based on clinical suspicion:
- D-dimer if PE suspected (Wells score ≥ 2)
- CT Aortography if aortic dissection suspected (unequal BP, tearing pain)
- Echocardiogram if pericarditis or tamponade suspected
- CT Pulmonary Angiography for confirmed PE workup

Troponin Interpretation (hs-Troponin):
- Baseline < 5 ng/L AND 1h delta < 4 ng/L → Low probability, consider early discharge
- Baseline ≥ 52 ng/L OR 1h delta ≥ 6 ng/L → High probability ACS, admit
- Intermediate → 3h serial troponin required

=============================================================
SECTION 3: RISK STRATIFICATION TOOLS
=============================================================

HEART Score (validated for ED chest pain):
H - History: 0 (slightly suspicious), 1 (moderately suspicious), 2 (highly suspicious)
E - ECG: 0 (normal), 1 (non-specific repolarization), 2 (significant ST deviation)
A - Age: 0 (<45), 1 (45-65), 2 (>65)
R - Risk factors: 0 (none known), 1 (1-2 risk factors), 2 (≥3 or atherosclerotic disease)
T - Troponin: 0 (≤normal limit), 1 (1-3x normal), 2 (>3x normal)

Interpretation:
- HEART 0-3: Low risk → 0.9-1.7% MACE at 6 weeks → consider early discharge
- HEART 4-6: Moderate risk → 12-16.6% MACE → observation, serial biomarkers
- HEART 7-10: High risk → 50-65% MACE → early invasive strategy

TIMI Risk Score for UA/NSTEMI:
Criteria (1 point each):
1. Age ≥ 65
2. ≥ 3 CAD risk factors
3. Known CAD (stenosis ≥ 50%)
4. ASA use in past 7 days
5. Severe angina (≥ 2 episodes in 24h)
6. ST deviation ≥ 0.5 mm
7. Positive cardiac marker

Score 0-2: Low risk (5% 14-day event rate)
Score 3-4: Intermediate risk (13% 14-day event rate)
Score 5-7: High risk (26-41% 14-day event rate)

=============================================================
SECTION 4: MANAGEMENT PROTOCOLS
=============================================================

ACS (NSTEMI/Unstable Angina) Management:
- Aspirin 325 mg loading dose PO (if no contraindication)
- P2Y12 inhibitor: Ticagrelor 180 mg OR Clopidogrel 300-600 mg loading
- Anticoagulation: UFH, Enoxaparin, or Fondaparinux
- Supplemental O2 only if SpO2 < 90%
- Nitrates for ongoing ischemic pain (avoid if SBP < 90 or RV infarct)
- Beta-blocker within 24h if no contraindication (HR > 60, SBP > 100, no HF)
- High-intensity statin: Atorvastatin 80 mg or Rosuvastatin 40 mg

STEMI Protocol:
- Primary PCI preferred if door-to-balloon ≤ 120 minutes
- Fibrinolysis if PCI not available within 120 minutes (door-to-needle < 30 min)
- Dual antiplatelet therapy: Aspirin + Ticagrelor (preferred) or Clopidogrel
- Anticoagulation: UFH bolus 60 units/kg (max 4000 units) during PCI

Non-Cardiac Chest Pain (after ACS excluded):
- GERD: PPI trial (Omeprazole 20-40 mg daily)
- Musculoskeletal: NSAIDs, rest
- Anxiety/panic: SSRIs, CBT referral
- Esophageal spasm: Calcium channel blockers, nitrates

=============================================================
SECTION 5: DISPOSITION CRITERIA
=============================================================

Safe for Discharge (all criteria must be met):
- HEART score 0-3 AND
- Two serial troponins negative (0h and 1h or 0h and 3h) AND
- No new ECG changes AND
- Clinical gestalt low-risk

Requires Observation/Admission:
- HEART score ≥ 4
- Any troponin elevation
- High-risk features on ECG
- Hemodynamic instability

Requires ICU:
- STEMI
- Cardiogenic shock
- Refractory arrhythmia

=============================================================
SECTION 6: PATIENT EDUCATION
=============================================================

Return Precautions (Return to ED immediately if):
- Chest pain recurs or worsens
- Shortness of breath develops
- Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
- Dizziness or fainting
- Pain spreads to arm, jaw, or back
